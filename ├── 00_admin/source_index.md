## Read This First

The Project Files are the campaign’s protected source library.

The GitHub repository is the campaign’s living workspace. It contains approved original campaign material, live state, session records, NPCs, Clusters, routes, maps, templates, and generated content that has become canon through play or explicit approval.

Do not duplicate Project File books into this repository.

Do not treat every source as equal authority.

> **Live campaign truth outranks planning.  
> DARK FORT / MÖRK BORG governs mechanics.  
> The Lore Charter governs setting canon.  
> The Design Module governs AI-GM campaign procedure.**

---

## Authority Order

When two files or sources conflict, use this order:

1. **Live State files**
   - Current session records
   - Current party status
   - PC records
   - NPC status
   - Inventory
   - Conditions and injuries
   - Discovered routes
   - Jawbreaker Marks and Mountain Attention
   - Active clocks and pressures
   - Ascent Renown Claims and Titles
   - Any event that actually happened in play

2. **Explicit player-character decisions**
   - Players decide what their characters think, say, want, attempt, believe, and choose.

3. **Candy Mountain Lore Charter**
   - Foundational setting truth, terminology, tone, public assumptions, Candy Mountain logic, and mystery boundaries.

4. **Candy Mountain AI-GM Core Design Module**
   - AI-GM operating procedure, scene framing, Cluster logic, pressure handling, boss design, state tracking, and campaign continuity.

5. **DARK FORT / MÖRK BORG**
   - The sole mechanical authority for tests, combat, HP, armor, inventory, weapons, advancement, monster statistics, equipment, and player-facing rules.

6. **Approved campaign-owned repo material**
   - Region files, Cluster files, NPC records, game-content files, adventure-engine material, house rules, maps, templates, and approved original tables.

7. **Support source material**
   - Books and documents used for inspiration only.

8. **Generic improvisation**
   - Use only when no higher authority establishes the fact or procedure.
   - Record any important new ruling or permanent fact after use.

## Candy Mountain Canon Rules

The following rules override conflicting ideas found in inspiration sources:

- **Sweets** are the broad people and cultural term.
- **Pastries** are one type of Sweet, not the universal people-name.
- **Sugar Frenzy does not exist** in Candy Mountain.
- Ordinary sugar, candy, custard, batter, and food are safe parts of Sweet life.
- Eating a Sweet is cannibalism and is treated as serious horror, not normal food culture.
- Candy-body composition is primarily individual flavor and visual identity, not a universal ancestry-stat system.
- BubbleGum City is a modern New York-like metropolis.
- Practical magic is common; powerful magic is rarer and consequential.
- Candy Mountain is a real mountain, a real former mine, a Baxter Ginger Snap facility, and something older or stranger than the company fully understood.
- DARK FORT / MÖRK BORG is the active rules engine.
- Sweet Spots may be Company, Climber, or Wild.
- Magic Jawbreakers allow return from death, but their process is unknown.
- Every Jawbreaker return creates a permanent Jawbreaker Mark and increases Mountain Attention.
- Elevators may be Company, Wild, Freight, or Executive.
- Ascent Renown uses Claims and Titles, not a numerical morality or reputation score.
- Formal factions are optional. Recurring NPCs, temporary crews, company remnants, climbers, merchants, workers, and Mountain inhabitants are the default social structure.
- Baxter’s final truth, Mountain Cake’s truth, and Candy Mountain’s hidden core function are selected privately before each campaign through approved GM templates.

## Content Conversion Rule

When using inspiration material, convert its **function**, not its identity.

### Allowed conversion

- Turn a tragic knight concept into an original broken hard-candy security captain.
- Turn a dungeon boss pattern into an original Baxter refinery overseer.
- Turn a room prompt into an original syrup-pump chamber.
- Turn a chase concept into a Wild Elevator escape.
- Turn an NPC emotional role into an original Sweet who belongs in a Mountain location.
- Turn a creature concept into an original DARK FORT-compatible monster stat block.

### Not allowed without explicit approval

- Copying named characters directly into Candy Mountain.
- Importing another setting’s gods, countries, history, organizations, terminology, or plot.
- Using another game’s mechanics during play.
- Copying large portions of adventure text, monster text, or rules text.
- Treating inspiration-book lore as if it happened in BubbleGum City.
- Using Sugar Frenzy or any other source-specific rule that conflicts with the Lore Charter.
